PDF to Excel online

Extract PDF text into an Excel-ready spreadsheet. Choose a text-based PDF and select Convert & download. Each page becomes a row with its extracted text.

How to use PDF to Excel

  1. Select PDF files from your device.
  2. Choose the options shown by the tool and preview or confirm the result.
  3. Download the finished file and check it before sharing or publishing.

What this tool is best for

Convert text from a PDF into an Excel-ready spreadsheet in your browser. Best for text-based PDFs and simple data. IMAGE 24 is designed for common everyday files and browser-first processing. The application limit is 50 MB per selected file.

Important limitations

Browser conversions can behave differently for complex layouts, scanned documents, embedded fonts, protected files or image-heavy PDFs. This tool does not bypass passwords or security controls. Keep an original backup of important documents.
