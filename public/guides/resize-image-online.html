How to Resize an Image Online Without Losing the Aspect Ratio

A practical guide to resizing JPG, PNG and WebP images by pixels or percentage, choosing output formats and checking quality.

Quick answer

A good resize starts by deciding the final use: a website hero image, a social post, an email attachment or a document. If you only need a smaller file, changing dimensions while keeping the aspect ratio is usually safer than entering both width and height independently.

For photographs, JPG or WebP can produce much smaller files than PNG. PNG is better when you need lossless graphics or transparency. After resizing, open the result at normal viewing size and check that text, faces and fine edges remain clear.

Step-by-step

  1. Choose a JPG, PNG, WebP or another supported image.
  2. Enter a width, height or percentage and keep the ratio locked unless you intentionally need a different shape.
  3. Select an output format and quality, then download and compare the result with the original.

Good practices

  • Keep an original copy of important files.
  • Review the downloaded result before publishing, printing or submitting it.
  • Use a browser and device with enough memory for large files.
  • Do not use browser tools to bypass passwords or other security protections.
